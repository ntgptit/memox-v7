Radius

Four values, and restrained on purpose: this is a study tool for adults at work, and heavily rounded surfaces read as playful rather than focused.

The steps climb with the size of the thing — 8 on a chip, 12 on a button, 16 on a card — so the corner stays visually consistent as the box grows. A single radius applied to everything looks tight on a card and bulbous on a chip.

Where each goes
TokenpxUsed by
--mx-radius-sm8Chips, badges, small indicators, a tooltip, a scroll thumb.
--mx-radius-md12Buttons, inputs, list rows, icon buttons, snackbars.
--mx-radius-lg16Cards and sheets — the review card itself. Dialogs.
--mx-radius-pill999Fully rounded: pills, the FAB, a progress track, the drag handle.
  • pill is a shape, not a radius. It means "the ends are semicircles at whatever height this is" — which is why the pill button and the FAB share it despite being nothing alike in size.
  • A sheet rounds only its top corners. The bottom two are off-screen, and rounding them leaves a sliver of page under a surface that is supposed to be anchored.
  • An interactive card clips its states to the same radius as its border. A state that overflows the corner is the single most obvious sign of a hand-rolled control.
