The gutter is not a constant. 16 a side costs 10% of a 320-wide screen and 8% of a 393-wide one — the same number and a different amount of the screen, which is what makes a narrow phone feel padded rather than laid out. Below 360 it drops to md, and MxContentShell resolves that so no screen has to.

Compact takes horizontal padding out of buttons and list rows and leaves the vertical rhythm alone. That is the opposite of what "make it smaller" would do, and the only version that helps: four review actions at 320 give each button 68px, and 24 a side leaves 20 of it for the label.
