Card prompt

The front of a review card — the one place the app deliberately gets large, because that text is the task. It is headline-medium, overridden from Material's 28 to 30 with the leading tightened from 1.29 to 1.22 and −0.5 of tracking.

Both overrides are for the same failure: a long-ish prompt otherwise wraps in a way that pushes the answer below the fold inside the phone frame, which is the one thing the review screen must not do.

Why compact shrinks this and not the body

Below 360 the prompt drops to 26. At 30 a two-word prompt takes three lines at 320 wide, and the answer goes below the fold.

Body and label text are deliberately untouched by the compact scale. Scaling readable text down by device width silently undoes the user's own accessibility setting — and it undoes it hardest for the people most likely to need it, since a large-text setting is at least as common on a small cheap phone as on a big one. What shrinks is the type the app chose to make oversized, and the padding around it.
