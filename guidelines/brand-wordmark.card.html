Wordmark

MemoX, set in Plus Jakarta Sans Bold at −0.02em tracking, with the final X in primary. That is the whole mark: the app has one visual signature and it is the display face, so the wordmark is the face used well rather than a drawing placed beside it.

The coloured X is the only place brand colour is used decoratively. Everywhere else primary means "this is the action", and a logo is not an action — which is precisely why the mark is allowed to borrow it once and nothing else is.

Clear space and the monogram

Clear space is half the cap height on every side — the dashed box above. It is expressed as a ratio rather than a pixel value so it holds at every size the mark is used at.

The monogram is for spaces the wordmark does not fit: an app icon, an avatar, a favicon. The tile is primary at lg radius plus a little, and the second letter takes inverse-primary so the two are distinguishable at 32px where the lowercase x would otherwise disappear into the M.

Do not
  • Re-space it. The tracking is part of the mark. "M e m o X" is a different logo.
  • Colour the X anything but primary — or, on a primary fill, inverse-primary. On the app's own elevated surfaces focus-ring is the one permitted substitution, because primary against surface-elevated in dark does not clear a graphic's 3:1 floor.
  • Set it in anything but Plus Jakarta Sans Bold. Not the body face, not a fallback, not a synthesised bold.
  • Put it in a box it does not need. The wordmark sits on the page; the monogram is the version that comes with a container.
  • Use it as a heading. The app name in running text is title-large like any other title — the mark is for chrome that identifies the app, not for a screen that mentions it.
