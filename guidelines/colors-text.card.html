Text

Neither end is pure. #EDEEF5 rather than white, #16182B rather than black: a pure value buzzes against a tinted ground, and carrying a trace of the surface hue makes text sit in the interface rather than on top of it.

Two weights of text, not three. text-primary is what the user is reading; text-secondary is everything that supports it. A third tier is how a screen ends up with a hierarchy nobody can see.

Rules
  • Body text clears 4.5:1 against the surface it sits on, in both modes. Every figure on this card is computed from the token, not asserted.
  • Metadata that carries information is not info. A streak, a counter, "3 of 20" — those are info. Plain metadata is text-secondary. Colouring everything quiet blue is how a study tool starts looking like a dashboard.
  • A disabled label is on-surface at 38%, computed with color-mix rather than pasted as a hex, so it stays right when the surface under it changes.
