Labels

Labels name controls. All three are Inter, and all three are heavier than body at the same size — a button's label at regular weight reads as text that happens to be inside a box.

label-large is semibold rather than Material's medium. It is the button label and the breadcrumb step, both of which sit on a surface with no fill behind them in the secondary and current cases, and 500 is not enough to carry them.
