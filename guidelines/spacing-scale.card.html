Scale

Six steps, deliberately. A scale wide enough to express "a bit more" invites per-screen drift, and the drift is what makes an interface feel unfinished long before anyone can point at a specific screen.

Every gap, pad and inset in the app comes from here. The list is exported as an array so a test can assert the scale did not quietly grow a step — which is the only thing that stops a seventh appearing the first time 20 looks better than 16 on one screen.

The six steps (bars drawn at 6×)
--mx-space-xs 4px
Between an icon and its label.
--mx-space-sm 8px
Between tightly related items in a row.
--mx-space-md 12px
Inside a compact control.
--mx-space-lg 16px
Standard screen padding and the gap between list items.
--mx-space-xl 24px
Between sections of a screen.
--mx-space-xxl 32px
Around a lone focal element — an empty state, a single card in a session.
Rules
  • No value outside the scale. Not 10, not 20, not "just this once".
  • The touch target is not on it. 48 is a floor, not a step — see Targets.
  • Steps are not a ratio series. They are the six distances the app actually needs, which is why md is 12 and not 16 × 0.75.
