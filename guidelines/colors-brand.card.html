Why the secondary action has no hue

secondary-action is under 20% saturation, and it is a separate token from primary rather than a lighter version of it. A secondary action sits next to the review verdicts, and anything with a hue there competes with the two colours carrying the user's actual decision. Keeping the tokens separate also stops the pairing that once shipped a label at 3.09:1 — one colour cannot be both a fill and a label on a dark surface.

focus-ring is the same hue as primary at the intensity meant to pull attention, which is why it does double duty as the spinner and progress colour. Material's default there is primary, and dark primary measures 2.81:1 against the surface it spins on — under the 3.0 floor a graphic needs. focus-ring measures 5.36:1 dark and 7.41:1 light.
