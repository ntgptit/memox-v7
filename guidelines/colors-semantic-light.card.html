Semantic — light

Four meanings ColorScheme has no slot for, carried as a theme extension rather than as globals — a global successColor is correct in exactly one brightness, and wrong in the other on every screen at once.

On a chroma budget, and deliberately not equal: danger is the alarm and carries the most saturation, info is only an indicator and carries the least. None reaches full saturation — four hues all shouting is how a study tool starts looking like a game.

Rules
  • Never colour alone. A red outline with no message tells a colour-blind user that something is different and not what. Every semantic colour in this app arrives with a word or a glyph beside it.
  • danger is also error. The Material role is mapped onto this token so the two cannot diverge, and the error/onError pair is contrast-checked against each other.
  • Empty is not error. "You have finished everything due today" is good news; rendering it in danger tells the user something is broken when nothing is. MxEmptyState and MxErrorState are separate components for this reason alone.
  • info is for status that carries information, not for decoration. Plain metadata is text-secondary.
